About Funke Furnishers

Our story, our craft, our promise to Malawi

OUR STORY

Crafting Quality
Since 2016

Funke Furnishers was founded in Lilongwe, Malawi with one simple mission: to make premium, handcrafted furniture accessible to Malawian families and businesses.

What started as a small workshop has grown into one of Lilongwe's most trusted furniture businesses, proudly serving over 500 happy customers across Malawi.

Every piece we create is built by skilled local craftsmen using quality materials. We take pride in our work — and it shows in every sofa, bed, and dining table we produce.
